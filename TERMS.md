# Terms of Use

Please read these terms before you use Shakti. By using the app, you agree to them. Shakti is a student project. Last updated: October 2026.

## What Shakti is

Shakti is a support tool. It helps you alert people you trust and volunteers near you. It is not the police, an ambulance or any emergency service, and it is not connected to them.

## In an emergency

Call 112 (or 1091 or 181 for women helplines). Do not rely only on Shakti. We cannot promise that an alert will reach anyone, that anyone will answer, or how quickly. The app needs internet, battery and location, and our free server can be slow or asleep.

## This is a demo version

Login uses a test code, messages to contacts are not sent as real SMS, and features can change, break or stop at any time.

## Your account

- You must be 18 or older.
- Give true information.
- Keep your phone and your login safe.

## Rules for everyone

We may pause or remove accounts that break these rules.

- Do not send false alerts or fake reports.
- Do not use Shakti to follow, track, threaten or harass anyone.
- Add only contacts who agree to be on your list.
- Do not try to break, overload or misuse the service.

## Rules for volunteers

- Volunteers are ordinary members of the public. They are not police, security guards or trained responders.
- You choose whether to help. Never confront anyone or put yourself in danger. If it looks dangerous, call 112.
- After you accept, you can see a woman's name and exact location. Use it only to help her. Do not share it, save it, or contact her afterwards unless she asks.
- Our team looks at your ID photo before you get alerts. This is not a background check, and it does not promise that a volunteer is safe.
